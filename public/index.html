A quick field guide.

Single player: choose a difficulty and click Start single player. No squares open until you click; your first click is always safe and starts the timer. Clear every safe cell to win. Use Play again for a fresh board, or Leave / Reset to change the difficulty.

Multiplayer: create a room, share its code, and both click Ready. Clear every safe cell before your friend. Each player picks their own difficulty. Your opponent’s numbers and mines are fully visible. Hit a mine and a 6 × 6 area resets and reshuffles; the race continues. First to clear their board wins.

Left click a covered cell to reveal it. Numbers count the mines in the eight surrounding cells. Empty areas open automatically.

Right click to place or remove a flag. On touch screens, turn on Flag mode.

Chord: press and hold a revealed number to highlight its neighbors. With matching adjacent flags, a mouse or pen press opens its unflagged neighbors; otherwise release to dismiss the highlight. On touch, complete the tap to chord. All unflagged neighbors open. Incorrect flags can uncover a mine.

Every round starts fully covered. Your own map is generated after your first click, which is always safe. In multiplayer, a mine hit clears revealed cells and flags in a 6 × 6 area and reshuffles its mines. Visible clues outside that area may change. The region shifts inward near edges and uses the available size on boards smaller than six cells. Random boards may require guessing. Press Tab to focus cells, Enter to reveal or chord, and F to flag.

Easy: 9 × 9 / 10 mines. Medium: 16 × 16 / 40. Hard: 30 × 16 / 99. Custom: up to 40 × 30.

Rooms expire after 24 hours. Leave during a match to forfeit.
