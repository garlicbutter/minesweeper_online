How to play

Clear every safe tile to win. Your first click is safe.

Left-click to reveal; right-click to flag. For touch, use Flag mode.

Hold a revealed number to highlight its neighbors. Matching flags chord on mouse/pen press, or on a completed touch tap. Incorrect flags can uncover a mine.

Multiplayer players choose independent difficulties. Opponent numbers and mines are visible. Mine hits reset and reshuffle a 6 × 6 area; nearby clues can change. Leaving forfeits the round.

vs AI uses a basic test opponent. Click Ready to start.

Keyboard: Tab / arrows to focus, Enter to reveal or chord, F to flag.
